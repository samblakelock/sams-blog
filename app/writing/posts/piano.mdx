We just released piano on Pickup Music, bringing our lineup to guitar, bass, and piano, with drums coming in 2027.

Here's how we approach teaching piano:

1. You don't need to be able to read the grand staff to study piano with us. You can get to playing the music you love quickly by learning shapes and a little bit of theory.

2. We teach chords, harmony, and what's going on behind the songs you love. You'll learn how to play the piano as an instrument, not as a party trick.

3. You'll stay focused with detailed, structured learning plans. No jumping around or pedagogical holes. Everything is defined for you to learn by playing.

Feel free to check it out [on our app](https://pickupmusic.com) and let me know what you think.
